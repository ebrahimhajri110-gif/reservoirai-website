THE CHALLENGE

Energy data is growing.
Engineering decisions must become smarter.

Modern oil and gas operations generate large and complex volumes of reservoir, production, pressure, and operational data. Turning this information into timely engineering insight can remain challenging.

Teams may work across fragmented datasets, analytical workflows, forecasting methods, and specialized software before reaching a decision.

ReservoirAI is being developed to bridge that gap — connecting data, AI, and petroleum engineering into a more intelligent decision-support workflow.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT

From proof of concept
to industry validation.

An initial AI-assisted production forecasting prototype has been developed to test the technical approach using synthetic historical production data.

It establishes an early foundation for data processing, forecasting experimentation, model evaluation, and engineering-oriented analytics.

The next phase focuses on improving the analytical pipeline, testing additional modeling approaches, incorporating stronger engineering features, and validating the technology with real or anonymized industry data.

LONG-TERM VISION

From Reservoir Intelligence
to Energy Intelligence.

We envision a future where energy engineers can interact with complex subsurface and production data through intelligent systems that combine artificial intelligence with deep engineering knowledge.

ReservoirAI begins with reservoir and production intelligence. Over time, the vision is to expand toward a broader Energy Intelligence Platform supporting increasingly complex engineering and operational workflows.
